AI AGENTS

AI that follows rules

Our AI steps read messages, decide what to do, and use your tools. Each one has limits, logs, and a human handoff.

# every HNova workflow, in short
def on_new_lead(msg):
    reply = ai.draft(msg, brand_voice)
    if reply.confidence < 0.8:
        return notify_human(msg)
    send(reply)
    crm.log(msg, reply)

def on_error(step):
    retry(step, times=3)
    alert(owner, step)

FAQ

Questions we get asked

How long does a project take?

A single workflow usually takes under a week. A connected system takes 2–4 weeks, depending on how many tools are involved.

Which tools do you use?

Mainly n8n and Make, with custom code in Node and React when needed. We pick whatever you can keep running at the lowest cost.

Is my data safe?

We ask for the least access needed, store credentials in secure vaults, and sign an NDA on request.

What if something breaks after launch?

Every workflow alerts us on failure. Projects include a support period, and care plans cover ongoing monitoring.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. You get the workflows, accounts, and documentation at handover.

Which time zones do you work in?

We're based in Pakistan and schedule calls in your working hours. Messages get a reply within 24 hours.
