Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026. Please review this policy carefully. If you have questions, email us at hello@hnova.tech.

1. Who we are

HNova ("we", "our", "us") is an AI workflow-automation agency operating at hnova.tech. We are based in Pakistan and serve clients worldwide. References to "you" or "your" mean any person who visits our website or contacts us about our services.

2. What data we collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to respond to enquiries and deliver our services:

  • Contact form data — name, work email address, project type, and the message you submit through our website contact form.
  • Email correspondence — the content of emails you send to us.
  • Usage data — standard web-server logs (IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamp). These logs are retained for up to 30 days.

We do not use tracking cookies, third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics), or advertising pixels on this website.

3. How we use your data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Reply to your enquiry within our stated 24-hour window.
  • Assess whether and how we can help you.
  • Send you a proposal, scope document, or project updates if you become a client.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketing companies.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — responding to business enquiries you have initiated.
  • Contract performance (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) — processing data necessary to fulfil a service contract with you.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR) — where applicable law requires us to retain certain records.

5. Data sharing and third parties

We may share your data with the following categories of third parties, strictly for delivering our services:

  • Email service providers — to receive and send emails (e.g. Google Workspace).
  • Cloud infrastructure — servers and storage used to host the websites and automation workflows we build for clients.
  • Legal / professional advisors — when required by law or to protect our rights.

All third parties we work with are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable law.

6. Data retention

We retain enquiry data for up to 24 months from your last contact, after which it is permanently deleted unless you have become an active client, in which case we retain relevant records for the duration of our engagement plus 7 years for accounting and legal purposes.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data.
  • Request a portable copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@hnova.tech. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We store credentials and sensitive client data in encrypted vaults with least-access permissions. Our website is served over HTTPS. No data transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we take commercially reasonable precautions but cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

HNova is based in Pakistan. If you are located in the EEA or UK, your data will be transferred to and processed in Pakistan. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent mechanisms where required.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and their representatives. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 16. If you believe we have inadvertently done so, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
